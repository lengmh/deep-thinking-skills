# Synthesis and stopping

Stop when available evidence supports a clear or conditional decision and the next action
would not materially change it. Do not keep reading merely to produce locators, a longer
process record, or a more elaborate terminal.

For an unresolved conclusion, distinguish:

- `unresolved_evidence_limit`: a discriminating fact is missing;
- `blocked_access`: a known next step needs unavailable access or permission;
- `stopped_budget`: an agreed cost or time boundary was reached;
- `invalidated_question`: the premise or decision frame no longer holds.

Give the answer, key grounding, scope, limits, reversal condition when relevant, and the
single next action with the highest information or decision value.
